# Physics Problem: Connected Blocks and Friction

## Problem Statement
A $5\text{ kg}$ block ($m_1$) is placed on top of a $10\text{ kg}$ block ($m_2$). 
* A horizontal force $F = 45\text{ N}$ is applied to the $10\text{ kg}$ block.
* The $5\text{ kg}$ block is tied to the wall (it does not move).
* The coefficient of kinetic friction ($\mu_k$) is $0.2$ for all surfaces.
* Find the acceleration ($a$) of the $10\text{ kg}$ block.

## Solution

### 1. Identify the Forces acting on the 10 kg block ($m_2$)
The $10\text{ kg}$ block experiences friction from two surfaces:
1.  **Top surface** (between $m_1$ and $m_2$): $f_{k1} = \mu_k \cdot N_1 = \mu_k \cdot m_1g$
2.  **Bottom surface** (between $m_2$ and the floor): $f_{k2} = \mu_k \cdot N_2 = \mu_k \cdot (m_1 + m_2)g$

### 2. Calculate Friction Magnitudes ($g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$)
* $f_{k1} = 0.2 \times 5 \times 9.81 = 9.81\text{ N}$
* $f_{k2} = 0.2 \times (5 + 10) \times 9.81 = 0.2 \times 15 \times 9.81 = 29.43\text{ N}$

### 3. Apply Newton's Second Law to the 10 kg block
The net force equation is:
$$\sum F = F_{applied} - f_{k1} - f_{k2} = m_2 \cdot a$$

Substituting the values:
$$45 - 9.81 - 29.43 = 10 \cdot a$$
$$45 - 39.24 = 10 \cdot a$$
$$5.76 = 10 \cdot a$$

**$a = 0.576\text{ m/s}^2$**
